Finding a reputable spray foam contractor is the difference between a high-performing, energy-efficient building envelope and a costly remediation nightmare. In an industry where application technique is just as crucial as the material itself, identifying true professionals requires looking beyond basic marketing.

This guide, brought to you by the team covering the SPFA National Industry Excellence Awards and the renowned Top Gun live spray contest, breaks down exactly how to evaluate, interview, and hire contractors capable of delivering award-caliber results for your residential or commercial project.

What competition-caliber quality actually looks like

When judges at the annual SprayFoam Convention evaluate applications, they look for consistency. True professionals deliver a seamless monolithic barrier. The foam should be applied evenly, without significant voids, 'pull-aways' from the framing, or excessive overspray. A top-tier installer manages the mix ratio and temperatures meticulously, ensuring the chemical reaction happens perfectly every time.
