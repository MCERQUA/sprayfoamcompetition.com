Frequently Asked Questions

Are you officially affiliated with the SPFA? expand_more
No. We are an independent reporting and matchmaking hub backed by Contractors Choice Agency. We cover the SPFA National Industry Excellence Awards and the Top Gun contest journalistically and use publicly available winner data to help consumers find top-tier talent.
Does it cost more to hire an award-winning installer? expand_more
Often, pricing is highly competitive with market rates. While top professionals command a fair wage for their expertise, their efficiency, zero-waste application techniques, and lack of callback issues usually make them more cost-effective in the long run than budget contractors.
What exactly is the Top Gun competition? expand_more
It is a live application contest held at the annual SprayFoam Convention and Expo. Applicators must spray a mock-up structure in front of judges, aiming for exact depth, consistent texture, and perfect structural tie-ins while maintaining strict safety protocols.
